Section 5 — Personas and Channels

Step 5 of the Blueprint defines 5 Personas that interact with the Movie Ticket Request application. Each persona represents a distinct role with specific responsibilities within the booking workflow.

1Moviegoer

The Moviegoer is an individual who uses the application to search for movies, reserve tickets, make payments, and receive booking confirmations. This persona benefits directly from the application's streamlined booking process and ticket management features.

2Box Office Agent

The Box Office Agent assists moviegoers with booking requests, verifies ticket availability, and handles ticket issuance or cancellations. This persona interacts with the application to process transactions and manage seating assignments.

3Theater Manager

The Theater Manager oversees ticket inventory, manages show schedules, and monitors booking statistics through the application. This persona ensures that ticket requests are fulfilled efficiently and that all business rules are enforced.

4Customer Support Representative

The Customer Support Representative responds to moviegoer inquiries, resolves booking issues, and facilitates refunds or exchanges within the application. This persona provides high-touch assistance, ensuring a positive user experience.

5Application Control Agent

Manages system-level operations, monitors application performance, and ensures smooth functioning of the booking platform.
